## Spacing, radius, elevation
- Base 8px (multiples only): 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 80, 160px
- Radius: 0px on everything
- Elevation: none — separation via surface color contrast and 1px hairlines

## Rules (do / don't)
**Do**
- Every heading UPPERCASE LamboType, 0.0230em — non-negotiable
- #ffc000 on exactly one element per screen (usually primary CTA)
- Alternate full-bleed dark (#202020) and light (#ffffff/#f5f5f5) sections
- Scale aggressively: 80–120px hero, 40–54px section openers, 16–18px body
- All radius 0px
- 8px multiples only
- Separate with surface color and whitespace, never shadows/borders

**Don't**
- No rounded corners
- No colors beyond #ffc000 and #917300
- No shadows, glows, or elevation
- No multiple weights (400 only; hierarchy via size/uppercase)
- No Giallo on more than one element per screen
- No lowercase/sentence case for display or headings
- No tracking other than 0.0230em

**Use cases:** luxury automotive, premium product showcase, cinematic editorial, high-contrast photo-led layouts needing industrial restraint and single-accent brand expression.

## Steal this
- "One accent element per screen" as a hard budget, not a guideline.
- Single weight, single tracking value, all uppercase — hierarchy purely from size.
- Dark/light full-bleed alternation for cinematic pacing.
- Hairline carousel pips (40 × 1–2px) instead of dots for a premium feel.
