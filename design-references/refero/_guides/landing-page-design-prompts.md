# Landing Page Design Prompts

These landing-page directions come from real website styles. Use the examples to decide how your page will show the product, establish reading order, and make one next step obvious.

## Prompt: Show the product behind the promise

The style document supplies the visual rules. Fill in the bracketed product facts before giving this task to an agent.

```
Design a landing page for [product] aimed at [audience] trying to [job].
Use the linked Webflow DESIGN.md for its white canvas, large type, framed
product imagery, and blue action. Place a real crop of [feature] beside the
claim it proves. Add [evidence] and one clear next step. If the product
image is only a concept, label it as such.
```

## Direction 1 — Product-led explanation

Webflow uses a white drafting-table feel with one blue mark; Unicorn Studio places its visual product against dark prismatic light; monday.com keeps a white workspace with softer colored cues.

- **Webflow** — white studio, black ink, one blue accent
- **Unicorn Studio** — aurora behind smoked glass
- **Amplemarket** — sunlit workspace with scattered elements
- **Arcade** — electric blue ripple on white
- **monday.com** — white workshop with pastel sticky notes
- **FLORA** — black velvet gallery, pure dark aesthetic

### Webflow details

The prompt-worthy choice is what the visitor sees before reading the claim: a product view, an interactive demonstration, or the work the product enables. Keep the visual and first sentence about the same outcome.

## Direction 2 — Editorial and restrained

Anthropic and Claude use warm paper, serif display type, and clay accents; Notion feels like a notebook; Vercel keeps technical typography on white.

- **Anthropic** — scientific field journal on warm background
- **Claude** — warm parchment printed artifact
- **Notion** — warm paper notebook aesthetic
- **Vercel** — typeset terminal on white paper
- **Monocle** — quality broadsheet on cream paper
- **Hims App** — spa brochure on vellum paper

### Anthropic details

> Borrow the reading rhythm, not a pile of fonts. Give the headline space, support it with one concrete proof point, and reserve the accent for a meaningful action or distinction.

## Direction 3 — Expressive and memorable

monopo saigon frames quiet type with iridescent light; GSAP uses green on a dark motion-led canvas; Playdate makes the yellow product itself the visual anchor.

- **monopo saigon** — liquid iridescence behind type
- **Gsap** — animated chalkboard in design motion
- **Playdate** — yellow Game Boy under museum lighting
- **Intercom** — warm cream editorial spread
- **14islands** — monochrome editorial gallery
- **Partiful** — confetti landing on white marble

### monopo saigon details

> Expression can also come from scale and imagery, as 14islands and Partiful show. State the single memorable device in your direction, then make sure the offer and next step remain legible beside it.
