## Guide: Prompt pricing pages around decision quality

A good pricing page helps visitors compare options, understand value, and feel safe taking the next step.

### Why pricing prompts need conversion context

AI agents often generate pricing sections that look polished but do not answer the real buying questions.

### How to use it

1. **Define the pricing model** — Tell the agent whether pricing is per seat, usage-based, tiered, custom, freemium, or subscription.
2. **Name the comparison criteria** — List the features, limits, audiences, and objections users need to compare.
3. **Add trust and edge cases** — Ask for FAQ, billing details, enterprise contact, trial terms, tax notes, or cancellation copy where needed.

## Comparison

The design should make the right plan easier to choose. Visual hierarchy should support comparison, not hide it.

## Trust

Billing period, included usage, limits, support, refunds, and enterprise terms can change the decision.

## Conversion

A free plan, team plan, enterprise plan, and usage-based plan may need different actions.
