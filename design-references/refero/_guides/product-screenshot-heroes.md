# Product Screenshot Hero Examples

Core principle: "The screenshot should help a visitor understand the product surface, workflow, or outcome. It should not be a vague mockup placed under a headline."

## Key principles

- **Purpose over decoration** — Product screenshots function as proof points rather than visual filler; be clear about what the image communicates.
- **Message through scale and crop** — Different framing approaches (full dashboard, close crops, stacked screens, mobile previews) each convey distinct narratives about the product.
- **Mobile considerations** — Large application images can deteriorate significantly on smaller screens without intentional responsive planning.

## Three-step prompt approach

1. Identify what the screenshot proves — workflow, quality, speed, collaboration, analytics, or output
2. Specify treatment parameters — crop, frame, background, shadow, scale, annotations, mobile requirements
3. Ensure the product image reinforces the headline rather than competing with it

"AI agents often add app-window mockups that look generic or too small to communicate anything," so explicit design direction is essential.
