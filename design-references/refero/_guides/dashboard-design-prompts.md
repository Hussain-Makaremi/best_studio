# Dashboard Design Prompts

These analytics and work-product references show three visual directions for data-heavy software: editorial, precise, and expressive. Study the typography and surfaces behind each direction before adapting it to your own data.

## Prompt: Turn a reference into a dashboard brief

The style document supplies the visual rules. Fill in the bracketed product facts before giving this task to an agent.

```
Build an analytics overview for [product] used by [role]. The main job is [decision or action].
Use the linked Steep DESIGN.md for its captured typography, color roles, and surface treatment.
Use our actual metric names and data. Include [required filters and actions]. Show loading,
empty, and error states based on our product requirements. Explain which visual rules you kept
and where the dashboard needs a different layout.
```

## Direction 1 — Analytics on warm paper

Steep places product charts beside a calm, serif-led story; Seline and Pirsch also let pale surfaces carry the data imagery.

- **Steep** — Serif analytics on warm paper
- **Seline Analytics** — Quiet analyst's desk on warm paper
- **Pirsch Analytics** — Sunlit paper notebook
- **Modern Business Intelligence** — Sunlit greenhouse editorial
- **Lightdash** — Violet pixel-grid on drafting paper
- **Basedash** — Midnight data terminal

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ink Black | #17191c |
| Paper White | #ffffff |

Typography: Signifier (display serif, 400) · Sohne

> Steep uses Signifier for display, Sohne for interface text, and peach #fbe1d1 as its only warm accent.

## Direction 2 — Darkness with structure

Linear builds depth from near-black surfaces and fine borders, leaving its product panels to provide the visual detail.

- **Linear** — Midnight precision instrument
- **Mercury** — Alpine banking at blue hour
- **Fey** — Nocturnal Bloomberg terminal
- **Axiom** — Terminal window at midnight
- **Retool** — Warm obsidian workshop
- **Dovetail** — Blueprint control room at midnight

### Linear details

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Void | #08090a |
| Carbon | #0f1011 |
| Ash | #62666d |
| Obsidian | #161718 |

Typography: Inter Variable (UI and headings, weights 300–590) · Berkeley Mono

> Use that two-voice approach when navigation and machine-like metadata must remain easy to scan.

## Direction 3 — One accent with a job

Ramp keeps most of the page black, white, and warm gray, then makes its yellow accent unmistakable.

- **Ramp** — Editorial finance desk
- **Attio** — Architectural editorial on white
- **PostHog** — Warm paper desktop pinned to cork
- **Airtable** — Warm workshop with color-coded elements
- **Huly** — Aurora through a midnight window
- **Runway** — Kraft paper ledger under amber light
