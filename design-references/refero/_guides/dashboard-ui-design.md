# Dashboard UI Design Examples

12 real website styles from analytics, workspaces, and operational tools, to guide visual direction selection for dashboard design.

## 1. Calm analytical hierarchy

Restrained palettes and clear type hierarchies; ideal when reading trends is the primary task.

- **Steep** — Serif analytics on warm paper
  - Ink Black #17191c, Paper White #ffffff, Mist Gray #f2f2f3, Fog White #fafafb
  - Typography: Signifier · Sohne
- **Seline Analytics** — Quiet analyst's desk on warm paper
- **Visitors** — White engineering blueprint
- **Pirsch Analytics** — Sunlit paper notebook

## 2. Dense work (scannable)

Balance brand expression with interface restraint for tables and repeated actions.

- **Linear** — Midnight precision instrument
  - Void #08090a, Ash #62666d, Carbon #0f1011, Obsidian #161718
  - Typography: Inter Variable · Berkeley Mono
- **Attio** — Architectural editorial on white
- **ClickUp™** — Hardworking dashboard on white
- **Notion** — Warm paper notebook

## 3. Operational (action-oriented)

Dark foundations or limited accents direct attention to critical signals.

- **Ramp** — Editorial finance desk
  - Highlighter Yellow #e4f222, Ink #0c0a08, Paper #ffffff, Obsidian #1a1919
  - Typography: Lausanne
- **Mercury** — Alpine banking at blue hour
- **LaunchDarkly** — Neon control room — dark cockpit
- **Dovetail** — Blueprint control room at midnight

## Implementation guidance

"Supply your own data hierarchy, filters, tables, chart labels, and loading or empty states."

### Three-step process

1. Identify the decision (monitor, investigate, compare, or act)
2. Choose visual register based on use case
3. Specify real controls and states from product requirements
