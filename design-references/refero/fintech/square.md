# Square
> "blue signal on white steel" — Signal Blue as the only chromatic element on white and #f2f2f2, 4px-radius buttons, 24px-radius editorial imagery, photography as the sole source of warmth.

## Typography
- **Square Sans Display VF** — weights 400, 500; sizes 16–86px; line-height 0.97–1.5; tracking -0.012em. Fallback: Inter Tight.
- **Square Sans Text VF** — weights 400, 500; sizes 12, 14, 16px; line-height 1.38–2.63. Fallback: Inter.
- **Cash Sans** — weight 500; 18px; line-height 1.0, 1.5. Fallback: DM Sans Medium. Used for all button labels.

## Spacing, radius, elevation
- Base unit 4px.
- Spacing: 4, 5, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 18, 20, 30, 33, 47, 60, 80, 113, 120px.
- Radius: inputs 4px, buttons 4px, pill buttons 20px, images 24px, icons 32px.
- Shadow: rgba(0,0,0,0.16) 0 1px 4px 0 (applied only to primary filled buttons). Cards are lifted by #ffffff on #f2f2f2 contrast, no drop shadow.

## Layout
- Max-width 1200px; section gap 96px; card padding 24px; element gap 10–15px.
- Hero: headline block left ~45% (62px / line-height 1.0, Carbon, -0.74px; 16px Slate Gray sub-copy; dual-button row at 10px gap), right-aligned 24px-radius image ~55%.
- Footer: inverted full-bleed Obsidian with white 50px headline and email input.

## Rules (do / don't)
Do:
- Signal Blue as the only chromatic element.
- Headlines in Square Sans Display 400 weight, -0.012em tracking, 50–86px, line-height 1.0–1.19.
- 24px radius to editorial and card imagery.
- Pair primary filled with ghost variant, 10px gap, shared baseline.
- 4px radius for buttons/inputs/nav; 20px for pills, 32px for icons.
- Lift cards through #ffffff on #f2f2f2 contrast, never drop shadow.
- Cash Sans 18px medium for all button labels.

Don't:
- No Carbon for button backgrounds.
- No drop shadows on cards, panels, or images.
- No 600 or 700 weight on display headlines.
- No semantic colors (green, red).
- Don't break the white canvas with gradients or colored section bands.
- No border-radius outside (4, 20, 24, 32).
- No letter-spacing on body copy.

## Imagery
Photography-led; warm, naturalistic editorial scenes of merchants and customers with phones and card readers. High-key lighting, candid hands-and-objects framing, tightly cropped to human moments and devices. No illustration, 3D renders, or abstract graphics. Sole source of warmth and color in the system.

## Steal this
- Single Signal Blue as the only chroma; photography supplies every other color.
- Cards separated purely by white-on-#f2f2f2 contrast; the only shadow is on the primary button.
- 4px-radius utilitarian buttons paired with 24px-radius imagery.
- Inverted #030303 footer with a big white headline and inline email capture.
