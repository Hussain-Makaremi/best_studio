## Rules (do / don't)
Do:
- ABC Ginto Nord 800 in all-caps for section headlines; letter-spacing -0.01em.
- Reserve #5865F2 (Blurple) exclusively for primary CTA buttons and interactive focus states.
- Feature cards with distinct gradient/solid backgrounds to differentiate sections.
- Pair the blurple primary button with a white secondary button (15px 24px padding, 12px radius).
- Position 3D characters at card edges, overlapping between sections.
- 12px radius on standard buttons, 16px on the Log In/header button.
- Fog (#BABCD9) for body text in dark sections; reserve pure white for headlines.

Don't:
- No white/light-gray page background; deep blue-black is non-negotiable.
- No ABC Ginto Nord in mixed case; all-caps and tight tracking are inseparable.
- No Blurple as background on large areas (it overwhelms the accent role).
- No gradient text on headlines; solid letterforms give the stamped-metal quality.
- No drop shadows on feature cards.
- No button border-radius below 12px.
- No Greyple (#99AAB5) or Dim Grey (#50555F) as primary text colors.

## Imagery
Entirely 3D illustration and product screenshots (no photography). Characters: cartoon-realistic 3D style with subsurface lighting, purple-blue shadows, saturated clothing, posed dynamically, off-axis. Feature cards use full-bleed gradient backgrounds as lighting. Floating 3D props at section boundaries. Zero photography.

## Steal this
- All-caps heavy rounded display at 0.86 line-height and -0.01em tracking, one weight (800).
- 3D characters overlapping card edges and section boundaries.
- Each feature card as its own "lit environment" via a full-bleed gradient or solid.
- Fog (#BABCD9) body on dark, white reserved for headlines.
